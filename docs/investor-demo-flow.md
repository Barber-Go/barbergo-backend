## 5 Hard Questions + Answers

**Q: "Why not just use WhatsApp?"**
A: WhatsApp has no payments, no tax compliance, no analytics, no loyalty points. We're building the Uber for barbershops.

**Q: "What's your TAM?"**
A: 45,000 barbershops in Chile. $15/month platform fee = $8.1M ARR at full penetration. LATAM expansion: 500K barbershops = $90M ARR.

**Q: "How do you make money?"**
A: 15% commission on in-app payments (Mercado Pago). Plus optional premium tax module. Plus future: supplies marketplace.

**Q: "Who's the competition?"**
A: Booksy, Vagaro — none have SII integration. We're the only platform that does reservations + payments + tax compliance for Chilean barbershops.

**Q: "What's your traction?"**
A: MVP complete with real SII integration (proven with live boleta emissions). Ready for beta launch with 50 barbershops in Providencia, Santiago.
